Sometimes life brings challenges that feel too heavy to carry alone. Whether you are navigating anxiety, loss, relationship difficulties, or simply a sense that something isn't right, counselling can help you find greater clarity and a way forward.

I offer a warm, non-judgemental space where you are free to speak openly at your own pace.

How I work

I work integratively which means I draw on a range of different therapeutic approaches, including psychodynamic, person centered and cognitive behavioural approaches. Each person I see is unique and I don't believe a one size fits all approach works. Therefore I will use different approaches and interventions depending on what you bring and how the therapeutic relationship unfolds.
