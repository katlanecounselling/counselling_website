Psychotherapeutic counselling for individuals

Life can bring many challenges that can feel too heavy to carry alone. Whether you have a specific issue you want to address, or simply a sense that something isn't right, counselling can help you find greater clarity and a way forward.

I offer a warm, non-judgemental space where you are free to speak openly at your own pace.

How I work

I work integratively which means I draw on a range of different therapeutic approaches, including psychodynamic, person centered and cognitive behavioural approaches. Each person I see is unique and I don't believe in a one size fits all approach. Therefore I will use different approaches and interventions depending on what you bring and how the therapeutic relationship unfolds.

Psychodynamic

Exploring how unconscious thoughts, past experiences and early relationships shape your current emotions and behaviour.

Person centered

Providing a non-judgemental space where you are listened to and accepted, helping to increase your inner capacity for growth.

Cognitive behavioural

Practical tools to understand and shift unhelpful thought patterns and cognitive distortions.

About me

I am a BACP-registered psychotherapeutic counsellor. I have 6 years of experience working in addiction, mental health and sexual violence services. I have worked with adults to help them achieve their recoveries, while also helping them with a wide range of concerns. I currently work in a sexual violence charity and work in a trauma informed way. Whatever you are coming to therapy for, we can discuss what will work best for you.
